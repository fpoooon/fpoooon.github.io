Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Artorius is software you run on your own computer. This policy describes what it does with your information, including data it reads from Google with your permission.

What Artorius accesses

  • Google Drive (read-only, drive.readonly): the documents in your Drive, so it can answer questions from them. Archives, audio, video and images are skipped.
  • Gmail (read-only, gmail.readonly), only if you turn it on: your messages, so it can answer questions about your mail.
  • Your Google account email address, to show which account is connected.

How it is used

Your Google data is downloaded to the computer running Artorius, stored there, indexed there, and used only to provide features you use: searching and answering questions from your own documents and mail. Artorius never modifies, deletes, sends or shares anything in your Google account.

Where it is stored

All data, including the access token Google issues, stays on the computer running Artorius. It is not sent to the Artorius authors or to any server they operate. Artorius has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Sharing

Artorius does not sell, rent, transfer or share your Google data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising. No human reads your data. Your data is not used to train any model.

Limited use

Artorius’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Choose “Disconnect” in Artorius to revoke its access and delete the stored token; deleting Artorius’s data folder removes the downloaded copies. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
