Artorius

A personal assistant that runs on your own computer.

Artorius answers questions from your own documents and mail. It runs entirely on hardware you control: the language models, the search index and your data all stay on your machine.

Google Drive and Gmail

When you choose “Sign in with Google”, Artorius asks for read-only access to your Google Drive and, if you turn it on, your Gmail. It downloads your documents and messages to your own computer, indexes them there, and uses them only to answer your questions. It never changes, deletes, sends or shares anything in your Google account.

You can disconnect at any time from Artorius, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
